Click To Request — FAQ

What is Click To Request?

Click To Request is an easy way for you to express interest in pins you would like to see in an upcoming Fins and Pins show.

How do I use Click To Request?

All you have to do is input your whatnot username and click the pins you’d like to be pulled! The Click To Request system will keep track of everything you’ve selected and send it to Fins and Pins to pull for their next show.

Why does it need my whatnot username?

Any username will do! Using a unique username helps find and keep track of individual interest in specific pins. Using your whatnot username specifically will be useful for future updates coming to Click To Request.

If I select pins, am I expected to purchase them?

No! Click To Request is like pre-requesting pins. It is an expression of interest, not an intent to purchase.

How do I purchase my selected pins?

All pins will be run individually over on whatnot in an auction format, starting at their current market value. Show date, time, and link are listed at the top of the Click To Request page. Don’t have a whatnot account yet? Click here to sign up now and get $15 off your first purchase!

Do I have to purchase the pins through whatnot?

Yes, all purchases must come through whatnot. Fins and Pins does not currently sell on any other platforms, so whatnot is the only way to purchase pins you’re interested in. Don’t have a whatnot account yet? Click here to sign up now and get $15 off your first purchase!

I want to trade. Is Click To Request where I should select what I want to trade for?

No. Click To Request is for pre-requesting pins for Fins and Pins’ next whatnot show. Whatever doesn’t sell on whatnot will be for trade at upcoming Florida pin events. Refer to Fins and Pins’ pinned monthly calendar on Instagram to see where and when they’ll be trading!

Do I have to copy and paste my total number of requests over to the Instagram post?

While not necessary, it is recommended. Pasting your shared comment on the Fins and Pins Instagram post will allow Fins and Pins to double-check and keep track of everything that people have requested and make sure they haven’t missed anything important.

What do B and N mean on a pin?

During and after the show (not before it starts): Blue means more than one person requested that pin; green means only one person did. B means the first request on that pin was before the show started; N means the first request was during the show.

What is the report issue button for?

The report button is for issues with your ability to use Click To Request—for example, when a pin is not selectable or a selection overlaps another pin. We want to know about those problems so we can fix them.

Why is the fuel bar not changing as I select pins?

This means that your pin has already been selected by another user and is already counted in the total of Pins Requested. You will still see your total number of requests updated as you select, but the Pins Requested bar is only counting individual pin requests, not duplicate requests.

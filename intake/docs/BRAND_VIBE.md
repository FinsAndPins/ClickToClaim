## In-person welcome (source of truth)

At trading events Steve welcomes people to the table with something like:

> Welcome to the table of Fins and Pins. This is my daughter. She goes by Fins and Pins on Whatnot and Instagram. This is her 12 books of chaos, over 4000 pins. As you find things you love, please let us know so we can pull them out and put them on a board so we can try to trade for them.

## What that actually does

- Enthusiastic, personal, proud of Lexi and the books
- Regulars wait for the full welcome on purpose
- Crowds form even at quiet events; people wait in line (sometimes an hour)
- Some guests come to the event mainly to trade with Lexi
- People hang around to talk even when they are not trading, because they like the vibe

That welcome is part of the in-person brand, not just a script.
